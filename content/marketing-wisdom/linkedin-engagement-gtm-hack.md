# LinkedIn Engagement Mining GTM Hack

## The Opportunity

Viral posts with high engagement (400+ likes, 1300+ comments) represent a goldmine of warm leads. People who engage with content about marketing automation are signaling active interest in the market.

## GTM Strategy

1. **Daily Engagement Block**: Reserve 1 hour daily for engagement, connect, and message outreach
2. **Automate Lead Scraping**: Build tooling to extract engagers from viral posts as warm leads
3. **Automate Outreach**: Part of the GTM workbench should include automated connection requests and messaging sequences

## Implementation Ideas

- Scrape LinkedIn post engagers (likers, commenters)
- Enrich with profile data
- Score leads by relevance (job title, company, activity)
- Queue personalized connection requests
- Follow up with value-add messages
